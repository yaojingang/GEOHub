# Resolver Contract

The registry is the single capability source. Resolution preserves the existing route fields and adds a typed `decision`; exact recipes also add `workflow`.

- A single intent selects the smallest matching active skill.
- `brand-baseline-lite` requires explicit discovery and diagnosis language. Its stable DAG is `discover` followed by `diagnose`.
- `content-campaign` requires explicit discovery and content language. Its stable DAG is `discover` followed by `content`.
- `brand-baseline-content` requires discovery, diagnosis, and content in discovery-first order. `discover` fans out to `diagnose` and `content`.
- `strategy-observation-loop` requires strategy followed by measurement. Its executable TaskPlan inserts human approval, publication-receipt, and engine-observation-bundle gates between those Skill nodes.
- A planned route is never runnable. A positive planned match prevents an active entry or workflow in the same request from overriding this boundary. In 0.6.0 this rule applies to `geo-publish`, which returns `geo-content`, `required_inputs`, and `closest_v0_artifact`.
- Unknown or fully negated language returns `abstain` with `runnable=false`. Broad explicit GEO capability requests may select the active `geo` selector.
- Multiple connected intents without one exact active recipe return `clarify` with alternatives and uncovered intents. A semantic score inside the configured ambiguity margin also returns `clarify`.
- Cached local FastEmbed scoring may supplement unmatched clauses. Runtime model loading is cache-only and never downloads. Planned candidates stay unavailable regardless of semantic score.
- Route input is limited to 8,000 characters and 16,384 UTF-8 bytes. Oversized input fails with a deterministic validation error.
- Clause boundaries and negation scopes are parsed once per request and reused across every registered intent. Sequence boundaries and bare Chinese `不` both use the same action lead-in matcher and the index compiled from all active and planned Registry intents plus a small governed set of request verbs. English `need|want|plan|intend|prepare` may lead directly to an action or use `to`; token boundaries exclude forms such as `wanted` and an unrelated `tool`. Chinese `不\s*(?:再\s*)?` stays one negation prefix when one to four governed `请|需要|继续|想|去|要|打算|准备|单独|仅仅|仅|再|只|光` lead-in tokens reach a registered action. Action resolution checks a direct Registry intent, a governed object article `a|an|the|一个|个` plus a direct intent, then a governed lead-in chain with an optional article. Forms such as `个性化` and an article without an immediate intent do not qualify. The recorded negation span covers the consumed lead-in, so its internal connector-like tokens cannot open another scope. A later connector after the negated action in `不X，再Y` starts the positive `Y` scope. Planned matches remain non-runnable and expose their registered `required_inputs` and `closest_v0_artifact`. Bare `no`, `not`, `skip`, `avoid`, `跳过`, and `避免` govern only their clause; lexical forms such as `not only`, `no-code`, `no.1`, `不断`, `不仅`, `不仅仅`, `不只`, `不只是`, `不单`, `不单是`, `不光`, `不错`, and `不同` remain positive across normalized Unicode whitespace. `不单独` is a negative form and enters the same Registry-anchored lead-in path.
- A governed sequence connector registry supplies `and then|then|followed by|然后|再` to clause parsing, workflow matching, and exclusivity exceptions. Every soft clause boundary, including contrast and replacement markers, opens a positive second scope only when followed by an immediately registered action. A request verb prefix must resolve through an optional governed article to a complete Registry intent; a prefix alone never opens scope. The single-character `再` is a standalone boundary, while lexical forms such as `一再` and `再三` remain in the current scope. In `no longer only X, then Y` and `不再仅X，然后Y`, the registry metadata retains positive `X`; ordinary `no X, then Y` and `不要X，然后Y` keep `X` negative. `and only`, `only`, `只`, `仅`, and `请` remain governed scope markers outside that exclusivity registry.
- One governed additive connector registry supplies scope parsing, workflow matching, and exclusivity exceptions. It contains bounded English `and also|also|and|plus` and Chinese `还要|也要|还需|同时|并且|以及|并|且|和|加|及`; `brand`, `also-ran`, `and-based`, `plus-size`, and Unicode-hyphen variants remain ordinary words. Workflow matching requires separator-only text between a single-character Chinese connector and the first action, plus an immediately registered second action after governed lead-ins and optional English `a|an|the`. A request verb cannot skip explanatory text to reach a later intent. The same left-side adjacency prevents `调和`, `涉及`, `增加`, and `合并` from cancelling an exclusivity negation. Explicit additive entries such as `also` and `还要` may open a new scope after a completed negative object, while plain conjunction entries such as `and` and `并` keep an ordinary prohibition in scope. All entries preserve both stages in `no longer only` / `不再 + 仅/仅仅/光/只` exclusivity exceptions. Active pairs may form an exact workflow and any planned match keeps the route non-runnable. Any earlier replacement connector `改为`, `转而`, `只`, `instead (of)`, `rather than`, or `switch(ing) to` keeps the first stage negative even when its replacement object is not registered; hard clauses do the same. Precomputed lexical-positive spans exclude raw `只` inside `不只/不只是/不仅只/不单只/不光只` and forms with an intervening `是`, including normalized Unicode-whitespace forms; `不单独只` remains negative. Every replacement `只` outside those spans must lead to a registered action. Connector classes and following Registry actions are precomputed once per request.
- A routing workflow step has exactly `id`, `skill_id`, and `depends_on`; dependencies refer only to earlier step IDs. Registry `orchestration.nodes` may add approval and typed external-evidence gates for TaskPlan compilation.
- Intent scoring uses Registry-compiled lexical patterns. ASCII intents require Unicode word and hyphen boundaries; governed Chinese compound suffixes prevent `发布` from matching `发布会` or `发布者`. Balanced straight/curly quotes and backtick spans are mention-only evidence and do not trigger routes; an unclosed quote safely masks the remaining text.

Resolvers must not infer a workflow from generic GEO wording, negated stage language, a reversed stage order, or two modes within one skill. Only `single_skill` and active `workflow` decisions may carry execution authority.
